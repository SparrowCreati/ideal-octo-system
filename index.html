Hi,
I'm Gwen.

SparrowCreati is my one-person creative portfolio and freelance identity. It reflects the style I love most: rounded forms, playful contrast, warm color, and visuals that still feel clean, intentional, and easy to connect with.

I'm based in Baguio City, Philippines, and I work as an independent freelancer through Upwork. This website is meant to feel more like my brand world — a friendlier, more personal home for SparrowCreati than a standard freelancer profile.
